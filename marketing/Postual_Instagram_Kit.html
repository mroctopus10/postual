🎬 Higgsfield Prompts for Animated Reels

These are ready-to-paste prompts for Higgsfield AI video generation. Each produces a 4-6 second reel suitable for Instagram.

Prompt 1: Star Rating Rising

For: Review management service promo

A sleek dark minimal background. Five large 3D star shapes in a row, initially grey and flat. The stars sequentially fill with a warm gold color from left to right, each rising slightly as it fills, creating a satisfying cascade effect. Below the stars, the number "4.8" fades in with elegant white typography. Smooth cinematic lighting, subtle reflections on the stars, premium tech aesthetic. 16:9 aspect ratio.

Prompt 2: Phone Google Maps Search

For: Educational post about being found online

A photorealistic smartphone floating against a clean white background. The screen shows a Google Maps interface with restaurant pins appearing one by one, popping up with a subtle bounce animation. The camera slowly orbits around the phone at a slight angle. One pin glows bright green, pulsing gently, standing out from the rest. Soft studio lighting, minimal shadows, Apple-style product photography aesthetic. 9:16 vertical format.

Prompt 3: Before/After Instagram Grid

For: Service showcase — social media management

Split screen animation. Left side shows a messy Instagram profile grid with inconsistent, dark, blurry food photos slowly fading out. Right side reveals a professional, cohesive Instagram grid with beautiful food photography, consistent branding, and clean layout sliding in from the right. The transition happens with a smooth wipe effect from left to right. A subtle green checkmark appears on the right side. Dark background, modern UI aesthetic. 9:16 vertical format.

Prompt 4: Data Dashboard Coming to Life

For: Results/analytics showcase

A dark premium dashboard UI on a laptop screen. Bar charts animate upward one by one, line graphs draw themselves from left to right showing growth curves. Numbers count up rapidly: followers going from 234 to 2,847, engagement from 1.2% to 8.7%. The camera slowly zooms into the screen. Soft ambient glow from the screen illuminates the dark surroundings. Corporate tech aesthetic with sage green accent color (#6B7F5E). 16:9 format.

Prompt 5: Food Plate to Instagram Post

For: Restaurant vertical — content creation service

A beautifully plated Portuguese seafood dish (cataplana) sits on a rustic wooden table, photographed from above. The camera slowly pulls back as a phone frame appears around the image, transforming it into an Instagram post. Likes and hearts float upward from the post. The phone tilts slightly showing the caption area with hashtags. Warm golden lighting, shallow depth of field transitioning to sharp digital aesthetic. 9:16 vertical format.

Prompt 6: House Listing to Social Post

For: Real estate vertical

A stunning Algarve villa with white walls and a blue pool, shot from a drone perspective. The camera smoothly transitions as the image gets framed into a carousel Instagram post layout with price tag "€450,000" and property details appearing with clean typography animation. Multiple slides peek from the right edge. The post mockup floats against a dark background with subtle shadow. Premium real estate marketing aesthetic. 9:16 vertical format.

Prompt 7: Empty Restaurant → Full Restaurant

For: Results showcase reel

A time-lapse style animation of a charming Portuguese restaurant terrace in the Algarve. It starts completely empty with chairs and tables set but no customers. Gradually, the restaurant fills with happy diners, candles light up, plates of food appear on tables, the atmosphere becomes warm and bustling. The transition from empty to full happens over 4 seconds. Golden hour lighting, Mediterranean architecture, bougainvillea on the walls. Cinematic, warm tones. 9:16 vertical format.

Prompt 8: Review Notifications Cascading

For: Review management service

A smartphone screen against a dark background. Google review notification cards cascade down the screen one after another, each showing 5 gold stars and a short preview text. The notifications stack with a satisfying bounce physics. A counter in the corner goes from "12 reviews" to "87 reviews" with each new card. The phone has a subtle glow. Clean, minimal, tech aesthetic with white cards on dark background. 9:16 vertical format.

Prompt 9: €5 Coin Multiplying

For: Meta Ads educational post

A single €5 euro coin sits on a clean white surface, lit with soft studio lighting. The coin begins to multiply, splitting into dozens of coins that spread across the surface in a satisfying cascade. The coins then morph and transform into small human silhouette icons representing customers, spreading outward in a ripple effect. Camera pulls back to reveal hundreds of customer icons. Minimal, clean aesthetic with subtle shadows. 16:9 format.
