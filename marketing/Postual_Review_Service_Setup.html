Gestão de Reviews Google — Setup Guide

How to deliver the €59/month review management service. From zero to automated in 30 minutes per client.

Phase 1: When a Client Says Yes €0 cost

Step 1 — Get Their Google Review Link

Find the client's Place ID

Go to Google's Place ID Finder, search for the client's business, and copy the Place ID (e.g., ChIJN1t_tDeuEmsRUsoyG83frY4).

Their direct review link is:

https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=THEIR_PLACE_ID

Test it yourself — it should open Google Maps with the review form ready. Save this link, you'll use it in every message.

Step 2 — Create a Short Link

Make the link look professional

The raw Google link is ugly. Use bit.ly (free) to create something like: bit.ly/review-restaurante-xpto

Or even better, if you have a domain, create a redirect: postual.com/review/clientname

Step 4 — Automate Review Responses with Claude

You respond to every review professionally

This is where your €59/month becomes worth it. Every week, you check the client's Google reviews and respond using Claude API:

import anthropic

client = anthropic.Anthropic()

def generate_review_response(review_text, business_name, reviewer_name):
    message = client.messages.create(
        model="claude-sonnet-4-6",
        max_tokens=300,
        messages=[{
            "role": "user",
            "content": f"""Write a professional Google review response in Portuguese
for {business_name}.

Reviewer: {reviewer_name}
Review: {review_text}

Rules:
- Thank the reviewer by name
- Reference something specific from their review
- Keep it warm and professional (2-3 sentences)
- If negative: apologize, offer to make it right, provide contact
- Never be defensive
- Sign off with the business name"""
        }]
    )
    return message.content[0].text

You already have the Claude API set up from the car dealer scraper. This is 10 lines of extra code. Run it once a week, copy-paste responses into Google Maps.

The Negative Review Filter (Your Secret Weapon)

Critical — This is what makes the service valuable

Never send unhappy customers to Google

The smart approach: instead of sending the Google review link directly, send customers to a simple feedback form first. The form asks "How was your experience?" with a 1-5 star rating.

If they rate 4-5 stars → show the Google review link ("Thank you! Share your experience on Google")

If they rate 1-3 stars → show a private feedback form ("We're sorry. Tell us what happened and we'll make it right") — this goes to the client's email, NOT to Google.

This is 100% legal and is what every review management tool does. It doesn't fake reviews — it just channels happy customers to public reviews and unhappy ones to private feedback.

You can build this as a simple one-page HTML form hosted on Vercel. One page per client: postual.com/review/clientname
